## First choose the tool

| Clue in the question | Start with |
| --- | --- |
| Mirror, real object, image location | Mirror formula $1/v+1/u=1/f$, $f=R/2$ |
| Refraction at a plane surface, apparent depth | Apparent depth $=$ real depth$/n$ |
| Critical angle, TIR, fibre | $\sin C=n_{\text{rarer}}/n_{\text{denser}}$ |
| Single spherical refracting surface | $n_2/v-n_1/u=(n_2-n_1)/R$ |
| Thin lens, image location | $1/v-1/u=1/f$ |
| Lens power, combination in contact | $P=1/f$ (m), powers add |
| Prism, deviation | $\delta=(n-1)A$ or $\delta=i_1+i_2-A$ |
| Minimum deviation experiment | $n=\sin((A+\delta_m)/2)/\sin(A/2)$ |
| Colours separate, dispersive power | $\omega=(n_v-n_r)/(n_y-1)$ |

| Magnifying glass | $M=1+D/f$ or $D/f$ |
| Compound microscope | $M\approx (L/f_o)(1+D/f_e)$ |

## Mastery check

Try each without looking at the answer first. Use $g=10\ \text{m/s}^2$ only where relevant; here use refractive indices as given.

### 1. Concave mirror image

A concave mirror of focal length $12\ \text{cm}$ has an object $18\ \text{cm}$ in front. Find the image distance and magnification.

<details><summary>Answer and reasoning</summary>

$f=-12\ \text{cm}$, $u=-18\ \text{cm}$. Then $1/v=1/f-1/u=-1/12+1/18=(-3+2)/36=-1/36$, so $v=-36\ \text{cm}$: a real image $36\ \text{cm}$ in front. Magnification $m=-v/u=-(-36)/(-18)=-2$: inverted and twice as large.

</details>

### 2. Convex mirror

An object is $24\ \text{cm}$ in front of a convex mirror of radius $24\ \text{cm}$. Find the image position and size relative to the object if the object is $4\ \text{cm}$ tall.

<details><summary>Answer and reasoning</summary>

$R=+24\ \text{cm}$, so $f=+12\ \text{cm}$; $u=-24\ \text{cm}$. Then $1/v=1/12-1/(-24)=1/12+1/24=3/24=1/8$, so $v=+8\ \text{cm}$: virtual, $8\ \text{cm}$ behind the mirror. Magnification $m=-v/u=-8/(-24)=+1/3$, so the image is erect and $4/3\ \text{cm}$ tall.

</details>

### 3. Apparent depth and TIR

A glass block of refractive index $1.5$ has a scratch $6\ \text{cm}$ below its top surface. (a) How deep does the scratch appear from above? (b) What is the critical angle for glass to air?

<details><summary>Answer and reasoning</summary>

(a) Apparent depth $=6/1.5=4\ \text{cm}$. (b) $\sin C=1/1.5=2/3$, so $C\approx 41.8^\circ$.

</details>

### 4. Thin lens image

A converging lens of focal length $10\ \text{cm}$ has an object $15\ \text{cm}$ in front. Find the image position and magnification.

<details><summary>Answer and reasoning</summary>

$u=-15\ \text{cm}$, $f=+10\ \text{cm}$. Then $1/v=1/10+1/(-15)=1/10-1/15=1/30$, so $v=+30\ \text{cm}$: real image $30\ \text{cm}$ on the far side. Magnification $m=v/u=30/(-15)=-2$: inverted and twice as large.

</details>

### 5. Lens power combination

A converging lens of power $+5\ \text{D}$ is combined with a diverging lens of power $-2\ \text{D}$ in contact. Find the focal length of the combination and state whether it converges or diverges.

<details><summary>Answer and reasoning</summary>

$P_{\text{total}}=5-2=+3\ \text{D}$, so $f=1/3\ \text{m}\approx 33.3\ \text{cm}$. Positive, so the combination is converging.

</details>

### 6. Lensmaker

A plano-convex lens has one flat face and one curved face of radius $25\ \text{cm}$. The glass has index $1.5$. Find its focal length in air.

<details><summary>Answer and reasoning</summary>

For a plano-convex lens, $R_1=+25\ \text{cm}$, $R_2=\infty$. Then $1/f=(1.5-1)(1/25-0)=0.5/25=0.02\ \text{cm}^{-1}$, so $f=50\ \text{cm}$.

</details>

### 7. Prism deviation and dispersion

A thin prism of angle $5^\circ$ has $n_{\text{yellow}}=1.52$ and $n_{\text{violet}}-n_{\text{red}}=0.018$. Find the mean deviation, the angular dispersion and the dispersive power.

<details><summary>Answer and reasoning</summary>

Mean deviation $\delta=(1.52-1)(5^\circ)=2.6^\circ$. Angular dispersion $=0.018\times 5^\circ=0.09^\circ$. Dispersive power $\omega=0.018/(1.52-1)=0.018/0.52\approx 0.0346$.

</details>

### 8. Minimum deviation

A prism of angle $60^\circ$ produces minimum deviation $30^\circ$. Find its refractive index.

<details><summary>Answer and reasoning</summary>

$n=\sin((60+30)/2)/\sin(60/2)=\sin 45^\circ/\sin 30^\circ=0.7071/0.5=1.414$.

</details>

### 9. Microscope magnification

A compound microscope has objective focal length $4\ \text{mm}$, eyepiece focal length $2.5\ \text{cm}$ and tube length $16\ \text{cm}$. Find its magnification with the final image at the near point ($D=25\ \text{cm}$).

<details><summary>Answer and reasoning</summary>

$m_o=L/f_o=16/0.4=40$. $M_e=1+25/2.5=11$. Total $M=40\times 11=440$.

</details>

### 10. Telescope

An astronomical telescope has objective focal length $80\ \text{cm}$ and eyepiece focal length $5\ \text{cm}$. Find its magnification and length in normal adjustment, and state whether the image is erect or inverted.

<details><summary>Answer and reasoning</summary>

$M=f_o/f_e=80/5=16$. Length $=80+5=85\ \text{cm}$. The image is inverted.

</details>
