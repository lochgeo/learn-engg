## First choose the tool

| Clue in the question | Start with |
| --- | --- |
| Force on a charge in $\vec B$ | $F=|q|vB\sin\theta$; direction from right-hand rule |
| Path of a charge in uniform $\vec B$ | $r=mv/(|q|B)$, $T=2\pi m/(|q|B)$ |
| Crossed $\vec E$ and $\vec B$, undeflected | $v=E/B$ |
| Field of a current element | Biot–Savart: $d\vec B=\frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\frac{I\,d\vec l\times\hat r}{r^2}$ |
| Field at centre of a circular loop | $B=\mu_0 I/(2R)$ (times $N$) |
| Field on loop axis | $B=\mu_0 IR^2/[2(R^2+x^2)^{3/2}]$ |
| Field of long straight wire | $B=\mu_0 I/(2\pi r)$ |
| Field inside long solenoid | $B=\mu_0 nI=\mu_0 NI/L$ |
| Field inside toroid | $B=\mu_0 NI/(2\pi r)$ |
| Force between parallel wires | $F/L=\mu_0 I_1 I_2/(2\pi d)$ |
| Torque on a current loop | $\tau=NIAB\sin\theta$ with $\theta$ to the normal |
| Galvanometer deflection | $\phi=NIAB/k$ |
| Ammeter shunt | $S=I_g R_g/(I-I_g)$ |
| Voltmeter multiplier | $R=V/I_g-R_g$ |

## Mastery check

Try each without looking at the answer first. Use $g=10\ \text{m/s}^2$ where relevant and $\mu_0=4\pi\times10^{-7}\ \text{T·m/A}$.

### 1. Magnetic force on a moving charge

A charge $q=3.0\times10^{-6}\ \text{C}$ moves at $2.0\times10^{4}\ \text{m/s}$ at $60^\circ$ to a $0.40\ \text{T}$ field. Find the force and state whether it does work.

<details><summary>Answer and reasoning</summary>

$F=|q|vB\sin60^\circ=(3.0\times10^{-6})(2.0\times10^{4})(0.40)(0.866)=2.08\times10^{-2}\ \text{N}$. It does no work, because it is perpendicular to the velocity at every instant.

</details>

### 2. Circular motion in a field

A proton ($m=1.67\times10^{-27}\ \text{kg}$, $q=1.60\times10^{-19}\ \text{C}$) moves at $1.0\times10^{6}\ \text{m/s}$ perpendicular to $B=0.050\ \text{T}$. Find the radius and the period.

<details><summary>Answer and reasoning</summary>

$r=mv/(qB)=(1.67\times10^{-27})(1.0\times10^{6})/[(1.60\times10^{-19})(0.050)]=2.09\times10^{-1}\ \text{m}$. $T=2\pi m/(qB)=2\pi(1.67\times10^{-27})/[(1.60\times10^{-19})(0.050)]=1.31\times10^{-6}\ \text{s}$.

</details>

### 3. Helix pitch

An alpha particle enters a $0.30\ \text{T}$ field at $30^\circ$ with speed $2.0\times10^{5}\ \text{m/s}$. For an alpha particle, $m/q=8.35\times10^{-9}\ \text{kg/C}$. Find the pitch of the helix.

<details><summary>Answer and reasoning</summary>

$v_\parallel=v\cos30^\circ=1.73\times10^{5}\ \text{m/s}$. $T=2\pi(m/q)/B=2\pi(8.35\times10^{-9})/0.30=1.75\times10^{-7}\ \text{s}$. Pitch $p=v_\parallel T=(1.73\times10^{5})(1.75\times10^{-7})=3.03\times10^{-2}\ \text{m}$.

</details>

### 4. Velocity selector

In a velocity selector, $E=3.0\times10^{3}\ \text{V/m}$ and $B=1.5\times10^{-2}\ \text{T}$. What speed passes through undeflected? Does it depend on mass or charge?

<details><summary>Answer and reasoning</summary>

$v=E/B=3.0\times10^{3}/1.5\times10^{-2}=2.0\times10^{5}\ \text{m/s}$. It is independent of both mass and charge, because both forces are proportional to $q$.

</details>

### 5. Field at the centre of a loop

A circular coil of $50$ turns and radius $0.040\ \text{m}$ carries $0.20\ \text{A}$. Find the field at its centre.

<details><summary>Answer and reasoning</summary>

$B=N\mu_0 I/(2R)=50(4\pi\times10^{-7})(0.20)/[2(0.040)]=3.93\times10^{-4}\ \text{T}$.

</details>

### 6. Solenoid and toroid

A solenoid of length $0.20\ \text{m}$ has $400$ turns and carries $3.0\ \text{A}$. A toroid of $600$ turns and mean radius $0.12\ \text{m}$ carries the same current. Find both internal fields.

<details><summary>Answer and reasoning</summary>

Solenoid: $n=400/0.20=2000\ \text{turns/m}$, $B=\mu_0 nI=(4\pi\times10^{-7})(2000)(3.0)=7.54\times10^{-3}\ \text{T}$. Toroid: $B=\mu_0 NI/(2\pi r)=(4\pi\times10^{-7})(600)(3.0)/[2\pi(0.12)]=3.0\times10^{-3}\ \text{T}$.

</details>

### 7. Force between currents

Two parallel wires $0.030\ \text{m}$ apart carry $12\ \text{A}$ and $8.0\ \text{A}$ in opposite directions. Find the force per metre and its nature.

<details><summary>Answer and reasoning</summary>

$F/L=\mu_0 I_1I_2/(2\pi d)=(4\pi\times10^{-7})(12)(8.0)/[2\pi(0.030)]=6.4\times10^{-4}\ \text{N/m}$. The currents are opposite, so the force is repulsive.

</details>

### 8. Torque on a loop

A coil of $25$ turns, area $0.0080\ \text{m}^2$, carries $0.60\ \text{A}$ in a $0.50\ \text{T}$ field. Its normal makes $40^\circ$ with $\vec B$. Find the torque and the potential energy.

<details><summary>Answer and reasoning</summary>

$m=NI A=25(0.60)(0.0080)=0.12\ \text{A·m}^2$. $\tau=mB\sin40^\circ=0.12(0.50)(0.643)=3.86\times10^{-2}\ \text{N·m}$. $U=-mB\cos40^\circ=-0.12(0.50)(0.766)=-4.60\times10^{-2}\ \text{J}$.

</details>

### 9. Galvanometer conversion

A galvanometer has $R_g=60\ \Omega$ and $I_g=0.50\ \text{mA}$. Design (a) an ammeter of range $3.0\ \text{A}$ and (b) a voltmeter of range $15\ \text{V}$.

<details><summary>Answer and reasoning</summary>

(a) $S=I_g R_g/(I-I_g)=(0.50\times10^{-3})(60)/(3.0-0.50\times10^{-3})=3.0\times10^{-2}/2.9995\approx1.0\times10^{-2}\ \Omega$. (b) $R=V/I_g-R_g=15/(0.50\times10^{-3})-60=3.0\times10^{4}-60=2.994\times10^{4}\ \Omega$.

</details>

### 10. Explain, don’t just calculate

Why does a uniform magnetic field not change the kinetic energy of a charged particle, yet a cyclotron can accelerate one?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

The magnetic force is always perpendicular to the velocity, so it does no work and cannot change speed. A cyclotron adds an electric field across the gap between the dees; it is the electric field that does work on the particle and increases its kinetic energy. The magnetic field only bends the path so the particle returns to the gap at the right phase.

</details>
