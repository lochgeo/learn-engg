## Choose the tool before you calculate

| The question asks… | Start with |
| --- | --- |
| “Give the IUPAC name” | parent chain → principal group → numbering → alphabetical prefixes |
| “Draw the structure from the name” | number the chain, place the principal group, then the branches |
| “How many isomers?” | DoU → skeletons → positions → functional groups → stereochemistry |
| “Which is more stable?” | carbocation: $3^\circ>2^\circ>1^\circ$; alkene: more α-H and more substitution |
| “Which is more acidic?” | stability of the conjugate base: −I and −M groups help |
| “Which is more basic?” | availability of the lone pair; resonance with a ring reduces it |
| “Separate this mixture” | volatility, solubility or adsorption/partition |
| “Find the percentage of an element” | match the data to Liebig, Dumas, Kjeldahl or Carius |
| “Find the formula” | percentage → moles → simplest ratio → divide by empirical mass |

## Mixed practice

1. Name CH₃–CH₂–CH(CH₃)–CH₂–CH₂–CH₃.
2. Name CH₃–CH=C(CH₃)–CH₂–CH₃.
3. Calculate the degrees of unsaturation of C₈H₁₀.
4. How many ethers have the formula C₄H₁₀O?
5. Which is chiral: 1-chlorobutane or 2-chlorobutane?
6. Which is more stable: but-1-ene or but-2-ene? Why?
7. Which is more acidic: phenol or p-nitrophenol?
8. A spot moves 2.4 cm and the solvent front moves 6.0 cm. Find $R_f$.
9. Calculate %N in urea, CO(NH₂)₂.
10. A compound is 40% C, 6.7% H and 53.3% O. Find its empirical formula.
11. What colour confirms sulphur in Lassaigne's test?
12. Why is steam distillation used for aniline?
13. What intermediate distinguishes an $S_N1$ reaction from an $S_N2$ reaction?
14. Distinguish homolytic from heterolytic cleavage in one sentence each.
15. Why is the enol form of acetylacetone unusually stable?

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. 3-methylhexane.
2. 3-methylpent-2-ene.
3. $(16+2-10)/2=4$.
4. Three: 1-methoxypropane, 2-methoxypropane and ethoxyethane.
5. 2-chlorobutane (C2 has H, Cl, CH₃ and C₂H₅).
6. But-2-ene; it has more α-hydrogens and is more substituted, so hyperconjugation stabilises it more.
7. p-Nitrophenol; the −M and −I nitro group stabilises the phenoxide ion.
8. $R_f=2.4/6.0=0.40$.
9. $28/60\times100=46.7\%$.
10. CH₂O.
11. Violet colour with sodium nitroprusside (or black PbS with lead acetate).
12. Aniline is steam-volatile and immiscible with water, so it distils below 100 °C.
13. A carbocation intermediate — $S_N1$ is two-step and unimolecular; $S_N2$ is a single concerted step.
14. Homolytic: one electron to each atom, giving radicals. Heterolytic: both electrons to one atom, giving ions.
15. The enol is conjugated with the second carbonyl and is further stabilised by intramolecular hydrogen bonding.

</details>

<learning-block type="mistake" title="The errors that repeat">

- Naming from the end that gives the substituent the lower number while ignoring the principal group.
- Counting cis/trans forms as structural isomers.
- Calling a meso compound optically active.
- Using Dumas and Kjeldahl interchangeably for nitro compounds.
- Forgetting to convert the mass of CO₂ or H₂O into the mass of C or H.

</learning-block>
